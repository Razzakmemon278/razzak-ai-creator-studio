# Daily Creator Workflow — Free-first and approval-based

This is a repeatable production checklist, not a claim that content is being generated or posted automatically while the owner is away.

## Daily deliverables

### 1. Islamic educational / tilawat channel
- Prepare one short idea or a 30-second vertical tilawat package.
- For Qur'anic recitation, use a verified, permitted recording from a named reciter. Confirm the exact surah/ayah boundaries and Arabic against a trusted mushaf.
- Never generate an imitation of Qur'anic recitation or present a fictional story as a verse, hadith or verified event.
- Package: verified source note, narration or recitation notes, captions, title, description and QA checklist.

### 2. Pip the Red Panda cartoon channel
- Use the 2-minute Pip episode document as the continuity master.
- Work in short clips; this episode is planned as 15 scenes of 8 seconds each.
- Package: scene prompts, voiceover, captions, title/description/hashtags, thumbnail concept and QA checklist.
- Do not report an MP4 as finished until the real exported file has been rendered and checked.

### 3. Fictional AI-character vlog
- Use a fictional character, not the owner's face.
- Prepare a 30–60 second vertical script with a two-second hook, simple scene plan, captions, and original narration.
- Check for impersonation, unsupported claims, privacy, and synthetic-media labelling requirements.

### Optional Pinterest affiliate deliverable
- Prepare one vertical Pin with an accurate product title, current checked price/specifications, destination link and clear affiliate disclosure.
- Do not invent prices, stock availability or product claims. Confirm the user's approved affiliate link is intact.
- Publishing stays manual.

## Suggested daily sequence

1. **Plan:** choose the day's topic and target channel; prepare scripts/prompts and source notes.
2. **Create:** generate or collect only the assets needed for the selected package. Check free-tier limits before starting generation.
3. **Edit:** assemble clips, balance voice/music, add captions and check mobile-safe margins.
4. **QA:** watch the full video, listen to audio, verify facts/rights, check dimensions, duration, watermark and final frame.
5. **Save:** store the real final file and the matching title/caption/hashtags/thumbnail package in a clearly named phone folder.
6. **Approve:** owner checks the exact asset and metadata.
7. **Publish:** only after explicit approval, using the relevant platform app. Never assume publishing permissions or post in the background.

## Free-first rules

- No paid API, payment-card trial or generation purchase by default.
- Free tiers are limited and change. Test a short clip first; no service is assumed unlimited or guaranteed watermark-free.
- Avoid uploading personal face photos, passwords, tokens or private files to public demos.
- Use a consistent naming pattern such as YYYY-MM-DD_channel_topic_v01.
- When the real asset, source verification or upload status cannot be confirmed, label it as a draft or blocked item rather than complete.
