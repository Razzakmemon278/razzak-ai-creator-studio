# Razzak AI Creator Studio — 40-Day Operating Plan

Status: working plan; publishing remains owner-approved. This plan does not promise views, virality, or income.

## Content lanes (keep separate)
1. **Tilawat Shorts** — short recitation clips with verified Arabic text, a trusted reciter/recording used with permission, accurate surah/ayah references, readable translations only when verified, and calm visuals. Never synthesize a reciter's voice to imitate a real person. Avoid product selling over Qur'anic recitation; use a separate end card/post if appropriate and respectful.
2. **Original 2-minute cartoon stories** — original characters, names, scripts, backgrounds, and sound; 12–16 short scenes; establish a hook in the first 2 seconds, clear conflict, payoff, captions, consistent character reference sheet, and a brief end card. No copied studio characters, logos, plots, or music.
3. **Affiliate product content** — product-first short demo/visual explainer, accurate specifications and price checked at publishing time, clear affiliate disclosure, no fake testimonials or unsupported claims, and a tested destination link. Keep this lane separate from religious content.

## Platform packaging
- **YouTube:** use Shorts for eligible vertical clips; 2-minute vertical cartoons may qualify as Shorts under current YouTube rules, but verify the upload's classification in Studio. Make a separate thumbnail/cover, concise title, useful description, relevant tags only, and correct audience/synthetic-media disclosures.
- **TikTok:** 9:16 export, safe-zone captions, strong first-second hook, native sounds only when licensed/available, disclose AI-generated content where required, and review privacy/draft settings.
- **Instagram Reels:** 9:16 clean export with no other-platform watermark, cover image, caption, accessible captions, and account eligibility checked.
- **Pinterest:** design a dedicated vertical pin (often 2:3 for static pins; video dimensions per current uploader), keyword-led title/description, accurate alt text, relevant board, and working destination URL. Do not blindly reuse every short as a pin.
- Repackage the same original idea per platform; avoid identical repetitive posts and mass-upload patterns.

## Sustainable 40-day test
- **Days 1–3:** audit profiles, rights, link destinations, account status, and analytics baseline; produce one test asset per lane; test private/draft upload first.
- **Days 4–10:** publish a small, consistent pilot (up to one post per lane per day across the chosen accounts only if quality and time allow). Compare retention, saves, shares, comments, outbound clicks, and production time.
- **Days 11–20:** repeat only the strongest hooks/topics; change one variable at a time; check platform-specific analytics after each batch.
- **Days 21–30:** improve weak openings, pacing, covers, captions, and product landing pages based on evidence. Stop formats with rights or policy uncertainty.
- **Days 31–40:** double down on repeatable winners; document conversion results and costs; decide what to continue based on actual analytics, not views alone.

## Suggested test cadence (America/New_York)
Use queue slots as test hypotheses, not guaranteed best times: 09:00, 12:00, 17:00 local. Rotate formats across slots and compare results. Do not post the same asset to all platforms at once by default. Queue currently has a product post scheduled for Pinterest + Instagram; check its status before adding a duplicate.

## Approval gates
Before every publish or scheduled queue change, owner reviews: final render, audio rights, script facts, spelling/captions, thumbnail, product claims, affiliate disclosure, target URL, platform destination, AI disclosure, and account selection. No profile changes, paid generations, or public posts without explicit approval.

## Reporting
Track per post: platform, format, topic/hook, publish time/time zone, impressions/views, average watch time or retention, completion rate if available, saves, shares, comments, follows, link clicks, conversions, rights/source notes, and next experiment. Evaluate earnings only from verified affiliate reporting or platform payout data.
