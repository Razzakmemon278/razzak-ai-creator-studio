# Media QA checklist

## YouTube cartoon episode (120 seconds, 16:9)
- [ ] Duration is 1:55–2:05 unless the brief says exactly 2:00
- [ ] All shots maintain the same Pip design: rust-orange fur, cream mask/belly, large dark-brown eyes, striped tail, teal backpack with one yellow star
- [ ] Story has a clear hook, rising action, payoff, and ending
- [ ] No unexplained character/clothing/location changes
- [ ] Watch full cut at normal speed and inspect transitions
- [ ] Speech/effects/music are intelligible and balanced
- [ ] No clipped audio, silent sections, frozen frames, visual glitches, or unwanted watermark
- [ ] Captions match the audio and remain inside safe areas
- [ ] Thumbnail, title, description, tags, and synthetic-content disclosures checked
- [ ] Set audience correctly; do not guess the Made for Kids setting

## Qur'an tilawat short (30 seconds, 9:16)
- [ ] Named reciter and permitted/verified source recorded
- [ ] Exact surah/ayah range confirmed from trusted mushaf
- [ ] Arabic text and ayah order checked character-by-character
- [ ] Audio is authentic recitation, not generated imitation
- [ ] Start/end cuts do not truncate words or tajwid
- [ ] Translation, if present, is labelled and checked separately
- [ ] No decorative effects obscure Arabic text
- [ ] Full 30-second clip listened to, not just sampled
- [ ] Reciter/source attribution and reuse permission checked

## TikTok delivery package
- [ ] MP4 vertical 9:16, readable safe-area captions
- [ ] Cover frame, title, caption, hashtags, and alt/context text ready
- [ ] No copyrighted music or misleading claims
- [ ] Direct download URL tested
- [ ] If no posting permission, hand over package; do not claim uploaded

## Pinterest affiliate pin
- [ ] 2:3 vertical image with readable product label
- [ ] Current product, price, specs, and link rechecked
- [ ] Affiliate disclosure included (e.g. “I may earn a commission from qualifying purchases.”)
- [ ] Pinterest board ID/access confirmed
- [ ] Destination affiliate link opens and is not malformed
- [ ] No publishing until owner approval
