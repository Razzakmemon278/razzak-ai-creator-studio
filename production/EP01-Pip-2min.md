## 120-second edit map
Create 12 scenes of about 10 seconds each. If the chosen generator only produces short clips, render scenes separately and join them in order. Do not stretch a 5-second test clip to pretend it is a two-minute episode.

1. **0–10s — Hook:** At a lively forest market, Pip opens a sandwich almost as big as his head. It suddenly rolls off the bench. Pip's eyes widen. SFX: soft comedic boing. No dialogue required.
2. **10–20s — Chase begins:** The sandwich rolls past fruit stalls. Pip hops off the bench and follows it, backpack bouncing.
3. **20–30s — Crumb trail:** Pip notices crumbs leading around a tree. Camera tracks at Pip's eye level; no sudden scene redesign.
4. **30–40s — Wrong turn:** Pip follows the crumbs into a tiny herb garden and gets a leaf stuck to his nose. Brief visual gag.
5. **40–50s — The clue:** A small hedgehog peeks from behind a basket, looking worried. Pip pauses instead of grabbing the sandwich.
6. **50–60s — Reveal:** The hedgehog explains it was trying to roll lunch to its family but the giant sandwich got away. Use simple, short English dialogue or captions.
7. **60–70s — Teamwork:** Pip offers help. They build a little twig-and-leaf ramp to guide the sandwich away from a puddle.
8. **70–80s — Almost lost:** The sandwich veers toward a gentle hill. Pip catches it with his fluffy striped tail; keep the action readable and safe.
9. **80–90s — Gentle rescue:** The hedgehog and Pip guide the sandwich into a small market wagon. Music lifts warmly.
10. **90–100s — Share the meal:** They bring the sandwich to the hedgehog family. The sandwich is cut into fair portions; friendly family reaction.
11. **100–110s — Payoff:** Pip realizes he has a tiny piece left. He smiles, shares it, and everyone laughs.
12. **110–120s — Close:** Pip waves to camera. On-screen text: “A little kindness goes a long way.” End card: “Pip the Red Panda — Subscribe for more tiny adventures.” Hold final frame 2 seconds.
