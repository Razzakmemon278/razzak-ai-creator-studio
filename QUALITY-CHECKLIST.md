# Creator Studio — Preflight and Rights Checklist

Do not publish until every applicable item passes.

## Asset and copyright
- [ ] Script, character design, images, video, voice, music, sound effects, fonts, and product photos are original or licensed for the intended commercial/platform use.
- [ ] No borrowed movie/cartoon character, logo, watermark removal, re-uploaded creator video, or near-copy of another account's distinctive design.
- [ ] Keep source URLs, license terms, and proof of permission where relevant.
- [ ] For Qur'anic recitation: verify the exact Arabic text, surah/ayah, recitation source and usage rights; review pronunciation/translation with a trusted source. Never invent religious quotations.

## Content accuracy and safety
- [ ] Opening 1–2 seconds clearly communicate the topic without misleading clickbait.
- [ ] Claims are supportable; product price/specifications/compatibility are current and checked.
- [ ] No fake reviews, fabricated results, guaranteed income/health claims, engagement bait, spam tags, or misleading metadata.
- [ ] Affiliate relationship is clearly disclosed in the content/caption as required; destination link works and matches the product.
- [ ] AI/synthetic-content disclosure is set where the platform requires it; realistic depictions do not mislead viewers.

## Video and accessibility
- [ ] 9:16 export, 1080x1920 where feasible, stable frame rate, no unintended black bars or other-platform watermark.
- [ ] Voice is clear; music does not mask speech; loudness is consistent; no clipping.
- [ ] Captions are proofread, correctly timed, high-contrast, and inside mobile safe zones.
- [ ] Character, outfit, voice, lighting, and style remain consistent across cartoon scenes.
- [ ] End card is short and readable. Keep affiliate CTA separate from tilawat to maintain respect and avoid confusing audiences.

## Platform and account
- [ ] Correct connected account and platform selected.
- [ ] Title, caption, description, hashtags, cover, alt text, board/playlist, and link tailored to destination.
- [ ] Audience setting is correct, especially made-for-kids settings for children's content.
- [ ] Privacy mode, scheduled time, time zone, disclosure settings, and queue status checked.
- [ ] Owner explicitly approves the final asset and exact destinations before public publishing.

## After publishing
- [ ] Confirm the live post renders correctly and the link works.
- [ ] Record post URL and initial metrics; review analytics after a comparable interval.
- [ ] If a rights, factual, or policy issue appears, pause further distribution and correct it.
