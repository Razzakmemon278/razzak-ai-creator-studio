## Episode promise

Pip notices a tiny seedling before a storm. His first shelter fails, so he adapts by building a simple roof from fallen twigs and a leaf. The seedling survives the rain and opens a new leaf the next morning. The message is to notice small needs, respond thoughtfully and return to care.
