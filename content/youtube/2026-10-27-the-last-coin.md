## 2. Voiceover Script (approximately 80–90 seconds)
**[0–8 sec | Hook]**  
A boy had only one coin left. It was all he had for dinner. Then he saw someone who needed it more.

**[8–20 sec]**  
On a cold evening, Daniel walked home with a single coin in his pocket. He was hungry, tired, and worried about tomorrow.

**[20–32 sec]**  
Near the bus stop, he noticed an elderly man searching the pavement. The man’s bus ticket had fallen, and the last bus was already approaching.

**[32–45 sec]**  
Daniel looked at his coin. He could buy a small meal with it. Instead, he stepped forward and paid for the man’s ticket.

**[45–58 sec]**  
“Why would you help me when you have so little?” the man asked. Daniel smiled. “Because I know what it feels like to need help.”

**[58–72 sec]**  
The bus pulled away. Daniel continued walking, still hungry—but somehow, his heart felt lighter.

**[72–86 sec | Payoff]**  
The next morning, the man returned to the same street with a warm breakfast and a note: “Kindness is never wasted. One day, it finds its way back.” Daniel had given away one coin, but discovered something worth far more.

**[86–90 sec | CTA]**  
If you believe small acts matter, share this story with someone who needs a little hope.
