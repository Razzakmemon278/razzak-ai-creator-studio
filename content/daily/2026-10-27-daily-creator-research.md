## 6) Research and tool watch

### Update 1 — Agentic AI can cost much more than a single-agent workflow
**Paper date:** 2 October 2026  
**Source:** [Engineering Sustainable Agents: A Systematic Comparison of Agentic LLMs for Developer Workflows](https://www.emergentmind.com/papers/2610.03010) (preprint)

**Plain-language finding:** In five software-engineering tasks, six open-weight models, two prompt strategies and three hardware platforms, the study reports multi-agent setups using an average **6.36× more energy** and taking **6.07× longer** than a non-agentic single-query baseline. Accuracy gains were task-specific; simpler configurations dominated 59 of 66 Pareto-optimal configurations.

**Limitations:** This is a preprint, not a universal benchmark for every current commercial model. Results depend on the tested models, tasks, prompts and hardware. The figures should not be assumed to predict every creator workflow.

**Action for this project:** Use one capable planner first; add sub-agents only for clear separate tasks (e.g., script, factual review, metadata QA). Keep review/test steps explicit and track retries so free quotas are not wasted.

### Update 2 — YouTube made realistic-AI labels more prominent
**Announcement date:** 27 May 2026  
**Source:** [Official YouTube Blog — Improving AI labels for viewers and creators](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/improving-ai-labels-viewers-creators/)

**Plain-language finding:** YouTube said its label for photorealistic or meaningfully AI-altered/generated content would be more prominent, including an on-video overlay for Shorts, and that its systems would begin using additional signals to detect significant photorealistic AI use. The announcement says a label alone does not change recommendations or monetization eligibility.

**Limitations:** The announcement describes a rollout; exact UI can vary by account, device and current YouTube Studio flow. Clearly unrealistic animation and minor edits are treated differently from realistic synthetic scenes.

**Action for this project:** Keep Kiko visibly stylized. For realistic AI-vlog footage, do not assume “it’s only a short” exempts it—review the disclosure option during upload and be transparent.
