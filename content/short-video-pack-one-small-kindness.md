## Concept
A young shopkeeper notices an elderly customer is short a small amount of money. He quietly covers the difference. The next day, the customer returns with a handwritten thank-you note. The message: small acts of kindness matter even when nobody is watching.

## Voiceover (original)
"At closing time, an elderly customer counted his coins again and again. He was still a little short. The young shopkeeper looked at the long queue, then gently paid the difference himself. No announcement. No camera. No expectation of a reward. The customer simply smiled and left. The next morning, a small note waited on the counter: 'You helped me keep my dignity. Thank you.' Sometimes kindness is not loud. Sometimes it is one small choice that makes someone’s day lighter. What small act of kindness can you do today?"
